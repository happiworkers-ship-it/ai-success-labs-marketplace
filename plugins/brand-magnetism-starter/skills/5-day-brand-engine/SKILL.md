# The 5-Day Brand Engine

**Created by Ngozi Cadmus — AI Success Labs**
**Part of the Brand Magnetism Accelerator: The AI-Powered Personal Brand Programme for Black Women**

The 5-Day Brand Engine is Ngozi's Monday-to-Friday LinkedIn content framework. It maps five posting days to three funnel stages — Awareness, Trust, and Conversion — so that every single week, your content is doing different jobs: attracting new people, building authority, and converting followers into leads and clients.

The hero in your story is always your client. Every post, every day, every week — the person reading should see themselves in what you write.

---

## The Framework at a Glance

| Day | Funnel Stage | Purpose | Content Type |
|-----|-------------|---------|--------------|
| Monday | TOFU (Awareness) | Problem-Aware Content | Make people aware of a problem or reality |
| Tuesday | TOFU (Awareness) | Personal Story / Community | Human connection and engagement |
| Wednesday | MOFU (Trust) | Education / How-To | Show expertise with actionable value |
| Thursday | MOFU (Trust) | Deep Value / Framework | Deepen authority and build saves |
| Friday | BOFU (Conversion) | Direct Sell / Lead Magnet | Drive people off-platform |

The weekly split: 2 TOFU, 2 MOFU, 1 BOFU. This ratio keeps you visible to new people while building trust with existing followers and converting the warmest ones.

---

## MONDAY — TOFU (Awareness) — Problem-Aware Content

**Purpose:** Make people aware. Stop the scroll with something that speaks to a problem or reality they recognise. This is discovery content — the posts that bring new people to your profile.

### What this looks like:

- "Why you need a personal brand in 2026"
- "Why personal branding is a lifeline for you as a Black woman"
- Contrarian takes that challenge conventional wisdom in your niche — these get the most attention because people are tired of recycled advice
- Industry insights and trend jacking — take something trending in the news and give your unique angle on it. LinkedIn gets news later than Twitter/X, so trends from other platforms are still fresh here. Use Relay AI agents to monitor trends and generate angles
- Start broad in the hook to pull people in, then narrow to your specific audience, then go niche in the solution

### Format:
Usually text-based with an image. Doesn't need carousels or infographics. The hook is everything — it must stop the scroll.

### Goal:
Get discovered. Someone who's never heard of you sees this post, finds it interesting, and thinks "who is this person?" Drive likes, shares, and new followers.

### Monday post examples:
- A bold statement challenging what everyone in your industry believes
- Your take on a news story or trend, connected to your expertise
- A "wake up call" post about a problem your ICP doesn't realise they have

## TUESDAY — TOFU (Awareness) — Personal Story / Community Post

**Purpose:** Human connection. Make people feel something and want to follow your journey. At least one day per week must be a personal story — Tuesday is that day.

### What this looks like:

- Personal stories that translate to your area — your dip-to-rise journey. Frame it through humility, not bragging. The "art of the humble brag" — make it about the journey, the family, the struggle, not "congratulate me"
- Promoting other people — "Black women in AI to follow", "Best people to follow in [your niche]". These posts get massive follower growth and invitations (columnist spots, podcasts, speaking gigs). When you promote others genuinely, it comes back to you
- Using other people's stories or achievements as examples — this shows authority while supporting your community
- If you've met or worked with someone credible, share the story with a photo. But only tag people who will actually engage — tagging people who won't respond gets flagged as spam by LinkedIn

### Format:
Text + real photos. A story without a photo doesn't hit the same. Photos of you, your family, events, clients — real moments, not stock images.

### Goal:
Engagement — likes, comments, shares, saves. Build the human connection that makes people want to follow you. Your audience should feel like they know you.

### Tuesday post examples:
- Your origin story connected to what you do now
- A "list post" championing others in your space
- A milestone or achievement framed through gratitude and the people who helped you get there
- A lesson from your personal life that applies to your professional expertise

## WEDNESDAY — MOFU (Trust) — Education / How-To

**Purpose:** Build trust. Show your expertise. Give people something they can implement immediately and see results. This is where you prove you know what you're talking about.

### What this looks like:

- Carousels breaking down a process step by step
- "How to use [tool] to create [outcome]"
- "These are the prompts that get better [results]"
- Practical strategies with specific examples — not theory, not fluff
- Give everything away. In a world where ChatGPT knows everything, the only thing you have is YOUR way of doing things. Share the full blueprint. If someone implements it themselves, they were never going to be your client. If they see the value, they'll want you to do it for them
- Lead with "how I" not "how to" — specificity builds credibility. "Here's how I did it" beats "here's how to do it" because it's rooted in experience, not generic advice
- This is monetizable expertise — knowledge that directly changes someone's outcome in a way they'll pay for

### Format:
Carousels, infographics, step-by-step breakdowns. Visual content that people save and share with their teams. Use Cowork for carousels, Dzine for infographics.

### Goal:
People walk away thinking "I learned something valuable I can use right now." They save the post. They share it in group chats. They start seeing you as the expert. Wednesday posts build the deep trust that makes Thursday and Friday content convert.

### Wednesday post examples:
- A carousel breaking down your process for [specific outcome]
- "The exact prompts I use to [specific result]"
- A step-by-step tutorial on a tool or technique your ICP needs
- An infographic showing your framework or methodology

## THURSDAY — MOFU (Trust) — Deep Value / Framework / Behind-the-Scenes

**Purpose:** Deepen authority. Show the depth of your expertise and give people a reason to keep coming back every week.

### What this looks like:

- Behind-the-scenes lessons — real experiences, mistakes you've made, what you learned. "I made X mistake last month — here's what I learned"
- Breaking down your frameworks with practical examples — show people your proprietary way of thinking. Framework posts get saved and shared more than any other type of content
- Case study-style education — "Here's what happened with a client and what we learned"
- If you have an offer with a framework or IP, this is where you showcase it — in an infographic, a breakdown, a visual that shows the value

### The Secret Tier:
Thursday is the best day to weave a BOFU CTA into a MOFU post. A lead magnet mention, a "DM me for the template" inside an educational post — this performs better than a blatant sell because you're giving value AND converting at the same time. When you put conversion inside trust-building content, you get more engagement than a straight sell post.

### Format:
Can be text-based, carousel, or infographic. The key is depth and actionability. The 0.1% of creators educate AND storytell simultaneously — the stories are so dense in education that people think they're learning, but they're retained because of the storytelling.

### Goal:
Build deep trust. People come back every Thursday because they know they'll get something valuable. This is where you convert lurkers into engaged followers who DM you.

### Thursday post examples:
- Your branded framework broken down with a real example
- "What I learned from [specific experience] that changed how I [relevant expertise]"
- A client case study (with permission) showing the transformation
- A "behind the curtain" post about your process, mistakes, or pivots

## FRIDAY — BOFU (Conversion) — Direct Sell / Lead Magnet / Social Proof

**Purpose:** Convert. This is the day you're direct about driving people off-platform. The exchange of value: they give you an email address, you give them a resource. Every DM is a warm lead.

### What this looks like:

- Lead magnet push — "DM me [keyword] and I'll send you the link." People DM you, you send the resource, they give you their email. That's a warm lead in your system
- "Comment [keyword] and I'll send you [resource]" — drives visible engagement on the post AND collects leads at the same time
- Testimonials and social proof — show results, client wins, screenshots of messages
- Case studies — deeper behind-the-scenes showing the transformation your offer creates
- Direct availability — "I'm taking on 3 clients this month", "I'm open for speaking engagements", "Spots are open for [programme]"
- Variations: "I just created this free guide on X — DM me 'guide' and I'll send it", "I've helped X people do Y — here's what they said"

### Important nuance:
You can (and should) weave lead magnet CTAs into other days too — not just Friday. You might mention your lead magnet once a week across any post. But Friday is the one day you're ALWAYS direct about conversion. Some weeks the blatant ask performs. Some weeks it doesn't. But you always show up on Friday with something that moves people off-platform and into your DMs or email list.

The goal of every DM exchange is to get an email address. LinkedIn is borrowed audience (rented real estate). Your email list is property you own. Every person who DMs you goes into your nurturing tracking system.

### Format:
Text + screenshots/photos of results. Or text + lead magnet graphic. Keep it direct but not desperate — you're offering value, not begging.

### Goal:
DMs. Email addresses. Conversations that lead to clients. This is where the nurturing system kicks in.

### Friday post examples:
- "I created [free resource]. DM me '[KEYWORD]' and I'll send it to you"
- A testimonial post with "If you want results like this, DM me [KEYWORD]"
- "3 spots just opened up for [offer]. Here's what's included"
- A social proof compilation — screenshots of results, DMs, or client messages

## Principles Across All Five Days

These apply to every single post, regardless of which day it is:

1. **The hero is always your client.** Every post should make the reader see themselves in what you write. Stories are never about you — they're about them.

2. **Personal story is woven through everything.** Tuesday is the dedicated personal story day, but your voice, your journey, and your personality should show up in every post. Authenticity is the competitive advantage — especially on LinkedIn, where corporate-speak is the norm.

3. **Lead magnets can appear any day.** Friday is the dedicated push, but a CTA to your lead magnet can be woven into Monday, Wednesday, or Thursday posts too.

4. **The "secret tier" is real.** Putting conversion CTAs inside trust-building posts (Wednesday/Thursday) consistently outperforms standalone sell posts. Give value AND convert in the same post.

5. **Engage for 30 minutes minimum after posting.** The first 30 minutes after your post goes live are critical for the algorithm. Be there. Comment back. Start conversations. This is non-negotiable.

6. **Spend minimum 1 hour daily on LinkedIn.** Posting, commenting on other people's posts, commenting back on your own, connecting. LinkedIn produces real conversion and money when you show up consistently.

7. **Minimum 20 new connections per week.** Some can do 20 a day — but at least 20 a week. Every connection is a potential client, collaborator, or referral.

8. **Comments are public auditions for your expertise.** When you comment on someone else's post, add insight or ask a strategic question. Never "great post." Position yourself as someone worth working with.

9. **Content repurposing is essential.** Only 10-15% of followers see any given post. Repost your best content every 60-90 days in different formats. Don't let good content die.

10. **Double down on what works.** Track your analytics — saves, impressions, engagement, DM conversations. The content that's working with your ICP? Do more of it. Not posting for the sake of posting.

11. **Quality always beats quantity.** Five intentional posts per week (Mon-Fri) that each serve a purpose within the funnel. Every post must earn its place on your feed.

12. **Use AI to create, not just think.** This is an AI-powered programme. Use AI tools to create carousels (Cowork), infographics (Dzine), LinkedIn posts (Claude skill), research competitors and find content ideas (Relay AI agents), and generate AI images. AI is a creation tool — that's the differentiator.

## How to Use This Skill

When someone asks "what should I post today?" or "help me plan my week":

1. **Check the day** — what day of the week is it? Map to the framework above.
2. **Ask about their niche/ICP** — what do they help people with? Who is their audience?
3. **Suggest 2-3 specific post ideas** based on the day's content type and their expertise.
4. **Remind them of the principles** — especially the hook, the hero being the client, and the CTA strategy.

When someone asks for a full week plan:

1. **Map Monday through Friday** with specific post ideas tailored to their niche.
2. **Ensure the TOFU/MOFU/BOFU balance** — 2 awareness, 2 trust, 1 conversion.
3. **Include at least one lead magnet mention** across the week.
4. **Include one personal story** (Tuesday).
5. **Suggest formats** — which posts should be text-only, which should be carousels, which need photos.

This skill works alongside the `ngozi-linkedin-posts` skill. The 5-Day Brand Engine decides WHAT type of content to create on which day. The LinkedIn posts skill handles HOW to write the actual post (voice, structure, hooks, formatting).
